# m01l02 · The Three Signals: Logs, Metrics And Traces

Module 1: The Foundations Of Observability · lesson 1.2 · Free · [Open the lesson](https://learnsome.tech/learn/observability-course/m01l02)

**Goal:** You can say what a log, a metric and a trace each answer well, what each costs, and which one to reach for when something is wrong.

## Labs

| Lab | What it is | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [m01l02-02](m01l02-02/) | A metric: cheap, aggregated, no identity at all | Read along |
| [m01l02-03](m01l02-03/) | A log: one record per event, full of detail | Graded |
| [m01l02-04](m01l02-04/) | A trace: one request, across everything it touched | Graded |

## Check yourself

- Which signal is cheapest at high traffic, and what does it throw away to be cheap?
- You are paged: which signal do you look at first, and why that one?
- What makes it possible to jump from a slow trace to the log lines it produced?
- Why do teams sample traces but rarely sample metrics?
- Give a question a metric cannot answer and say which signal answers it.
