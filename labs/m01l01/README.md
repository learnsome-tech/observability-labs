# m01l01 · Observability Versus Monitoring

Module 1: The Foundations Of Observability · lesson 1.1 · Free · [Open the lesson](https://learnsome.tech/learn/observability-course/m01l01)

**Goal:** You can say what monitoring is good at, what observability adds, and why a green health check is not evidence that a service is working.

## Labs

| Lab | What it is | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [m01l01-04](m01l01-04/) | A health check that says yes while work is failing | Runs, not graded |

## Check yourself

- In one sentence, what is the difference between monitoring and observability?
- Why is a dashboard unable to help with a genuinely new kind of failure?
- What does the deploy test ask, and what does failing it tell you?
- Why can a health endpoint report ok while customers are getting errors?
- Name the three kinds of telemetry this course will make the service emit.
